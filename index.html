Research

Research Statement

I investigate the formation and vertical structure of clouds in giant planet and exoplanet atmospheres through microphysical models that balance turbulent mixing with particle sedimentation.

My research focuses on understanding cloud formation and evolution in planetary atmospheres using one-dimensional cloud microphysics models based on the Ackerman & Marley framework. By combining realistic pressure–temperature profiles with sedimentation and turbulent mixing processes, I study condensate abundance, particle size distributions, and cloud thickness in giant planets and exoplanets.
